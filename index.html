01 — from my heart

A letter I couldn't fit in a text.

My love,

There are probably a thousand ways I could tell you that I love you, and somehow none of them ever feel like enough. So instead of trying to find the perfect words, I wanted to make you something you could come back to whenever you miss me.

Being far away from you isn't always easy. There are moments when I wish I could simply turn up at your door, steal a hug, and sit next to you without a screen between us. But even with all those kilometres between us, you've become such a beautiful part of my everyday life.

I love the little things. Your messages. Your jokes. The way you annoy me just because you can. The moments where we're doing absolutely nothing and somehow I still feel lucky that it's you on the other side of the phone.

And if there's one thing I want you to know, it's this: distance doesn't make me less certain about you. If anything, it makes me more certain that you're someone I want beside me when the distance is finally gone.

Until then, I'll keep choosing you — one day, one call, one stupid joke, one “goodnight” at a time.

Always yours, ♡
